Important disclosure

This app copies received text SMS—including financial, recovery, and OTP messages when Android delivers them—to another number you choose.

Forwarded messages may incur carrier charges on your outbound line. The recipient sees your selected outbound SIM as the sender.

Timely OTP forwarding requires the private sensitive-SMS install privilege. If the platform still delays OTP delivery, this setup is not supported without a documented companion exemption.
